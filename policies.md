# Juniper Supply Co. Help Center

## Refund policy
Delivered items can be refunded within 30 days of delivery. Each order can be refunded once, up to the amount paid. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Orders that have not been delivered cannot be refunded yet; they can be cancelled instead by a team member.

## Large refunds
Refunds over $100 are reviewed and approved by a support team member before they are issued. Let the customer know a teammate is reviewing the request.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or stops working within 30 days of delivery, the customer can choose a full refund or a free replacement. Photos are helpful but not required.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days. Customers can see the estimated delivery date for orders that are in transit. Orders in "processing" have not shipped yet.

## Returns after 30 days
Items delivered more than 30 days ago are outside the refund window. Support can escalate special cases, such as a defect covered by the manufacturer warranty, to a team member, but cannot promise an exception.

## Account and security
Support never changes account emails or payment details over chat. Support only discusses and acts on orders that belong to the customer who contacted us.
